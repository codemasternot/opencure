Transparent planning

Make the expensive parts of translational research visible.

AI inference is only a small part of the total research budget. Sequencing, expert review, synthesis, formulation, assays, quality control, and regulated validation can dominate the cost of a personalized research case.

These are prototype planning ranges from the OpenCure concept, not quotations or promises. Actual costs depend on the laboratory, geography, study design, depth, quality requirements, and regulatory setting.

OpenCure's prototype objective is to keep AI orchestration inexpensive relative to the physical research work, so more funding reaches sequencing, synthesis, assays, and validation.

How funding could be structured.

Instead of an opaque single budget, programs can be divided into evidence-bearing milestones.

01

Compute milestone

Fund data preparation, model runs, independent scoring, and a reproducible candidate report.

02

Validation milestone

Release funding for agreed assays, controls, replication, and documented negative as well as positive findings.

03

Translation milestone

Qualified partners define any subsequent preclinical, clinical, veterinary, ethics, or regulatory work.
