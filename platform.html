AI research platform

Multiple models. Independent checks. Reproducible evidence.

OpenCure RNA is designed as an orchestration layer for translational research: specialized models generate and rank hypotheses, uncertainty is surfaced, and only reviewable evidence packages move toward laboratory validation.

See the research workflow

One candidate, examined from several angles.

Rather than treating a single model output as an answer, the platform separates generation, scoring, structure-aware review, safety filtering, and evidence synthesis.

01

Candidate generation

Sequence-aware systems identify tumor-linked targets and candidate RNA designs under explicit biological and manufacturability constraints.

02

Cross-model scoring

Independent models assess binding, expression, structural plausibility, immunogenicity, uncertainty, and potential failure modes.

03

Evidence packaging

Each promoted hypothesis carries its assumptions, model disagreement, provenance, confidence signals, and a proposed validation plan.

Research infrastructure, not an automated treatment engine.

Candidate outputs remain investigational. They are intended to help qualified researchers prioritize work, not to replace wet-lab science, clinical judgment, ethics review, or regulatory processes.

Uncertainty firstDiscordant predictions are retained and surfaced instead of averaged away.

Designed to make disagreement useful.
