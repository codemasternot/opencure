RNA vaccine research workflow

From patient-derived signal to testable candidate hypothesis.

The goal is to reduce time wasted on weak leads, not to replace clinical science. Candidate designs remain hypotheses until validated by qualified laboratories and regulated clinical studies.

1
Ingest de-identified molecular dataVariant calls, expression context, and reference proteome data are prepared for analysis.
2
Generate RNA vaccine candidatesSpecialist models propose target sets, RNA constructs, and delivery-aware constraints.
3
Score across independent modelsDiscordant predictions are flagged while consensus candidates move into expert review.
4
Publish evidence packagesPartners receive reproducible reports, assumptions, failure modes, and lab validation plans.

A privacy-first blockchain layer for research trust.

Stellar/XLM can support low-cost donations, grant disbursements, and public verification while hospitals keep patient identity, medical records, and genomic data off-chain.

On-chain proof, off-chain medicine

Patient identity is never written to the ledger.

A hospital creates a private patient ID, mixes it with a hospital-held secret salt, and stores only the resulting hash with the research case record.

patientHash = SHA256(patientPrivateId + hospitalSecretSalt)
vaccineFileHash = SHA256(encryptedVaccineDesignFile)
  • ✓Doctor and hospital wallets sign each case action.
  • ✓Vaccine design files remain encrypted in secure off-chain storage.
  • ✓Authorized parties can verify the downloaded file has not changed.
