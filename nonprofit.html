Public-interest research

Build infrastructure that serves the research mission first.

OpenCure RNA is conceived as a nonprofit research initiative focused on transparent methods, reusable infrastructure, responsible claims, and collaboration with qualified scientific and clinical partners.

Support the mission

Cancer vaccine discovery should be faster, more transparent, and more accessible to researchers working outside the richest institutions.

What public-interest governance should protect.

The goal is not just to make research open; it is to make incentives, claims, and funding easier to scrutinize.

01

Scientific integrity

Methods, limitations, validation criteria, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest should be documented clearly enough for independent review.

02

Patient privacy

Open methods do not imply open medical records. Sensitive patient-linked information remains protected under appropriate governance and access controls.

03

Funding transparency

Donors and partners should be able to understand what a program is funding and what evidence was produced without exposing protected research data.

Origin of the concept.

The OpenCure concept credits the reported experimental veterinary mRNA vaccine case associated with Paul Conyngham and Rosie as an inspiration for asking whether AI and open research infrastructure can help accelerate careful scientific investigation. It is treated as a motivating signal, not proof of a human therapy.
