Research intake

Submit paired healthy and cancer genomic sequence data.

Doctors, laboratories, researchers, and individuals can prepare a paired normal/tumor sequence submission for OpenCure RNA research review. The goal is to compare cancer-associated variation against the person’s healthy reference sample.

Privacy requirement: do not put names, national IDs, medical-record numbers, addresses, dates of birth, or other direct identifiers in filenames or metadata. Genomic data itself remains highly sensitive and potentially identifying.

What happens to the files?

At this stage the page validates the paired file selection in the browser. The files are not transmitted yet because OpenCure still needs a dedicated encrypted genomic-data storage endpoint with access control, audit logging, retention rules, and research-consent handling.
